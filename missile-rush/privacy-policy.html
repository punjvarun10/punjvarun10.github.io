Privacy Policy for Missile Rush: Jet Escape

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Missile Rush: Jet Escape ("the app") is a free game made by Varun Punj ("we"). This policy explains what information is collected when you use the app.

Information we collect

We do not ask for, collect or store any personal information such as your name, email or contacts. Your best score and game settings are saved on your device. If you have turned on Android's device backup, they may be included in your personal Google backup, which we cannot access.

Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • information about your device and how you interact with ads,
  • diagnostic and crash information related to the ads SDK.

Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time with "Privacy settings" on the app's main menu.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new date.
